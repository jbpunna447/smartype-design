Getting started

Is SmarType on iPhone?

Not yet. SmarType is on Google Play for Android 8.0 and later, and the iPhone app is coming soon to the App Store.

How do I turn on SmarType?

Open SmarType and follow the two steps: allow it in Android's keyboard settings, then choose it as your keyboard. The app moves on by itself when each step is done.

How do I switch back to my old keyboard?

Long-press the globe key, or tap the keyboard icon in the navigation bar, and pick another keyboard.

Do I need an account?

No. Typing and suggestions work without one. Sign in with Google if you want AI features (Rewrite, Translate, Format, Notes AI and AI voice typing) or Premium.

AI features

What happens to the text or voice audio I send to AI?

It goes over an encrypted connection to our server and our AI provider only to produce your result, and isn't stored after the request. See the privacy policy.

How many free tries do I get?

Signed-in users get a set number of free AI tries; the app shows how many are left. Premium removes the limit.

Why doesn't AI work in password fields?

On purpose: SmarType never sends or learns anything typed in a password field.

Languages and voice

Which languages can I type in?

English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali, Urdu and many more. Add one in Languages and English stays in the list, so the globe key switches between English and your language.

Which languages can Translate write in?

31 languages: Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu, and English, Arabic, Chinese (Simplified), Dutch, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, Thai, Turkish and Vietnamese. It detects the language you wrote in, so there is nothing to set first.

How do I keep my languages in Translate?

Open Translate, tap More, search for a language and add it. Added languages show as chips next to English, so they are one tap away next time.

What happens if I pause while speaking?

Nothing: SmarType keeps listening and shows Still listening. Recording ends only when you tap Check, tap Delete or reach 60 seconds.

Does voice typing send my audio?

SmarType sends audio only when you use SmarType AI voice typing and tap Check. SmarType records up to 60 seconds in memory, deletes the audio immediately if you cancel, and sends it over an encrypted connection to our server and Gemini only to return your text — it isn't stored after the request. When you're offline or out of free tries, SmarType switches to Android's system voice input when available (handled by that voice provider under its own privacy terms without SmarType's Check step), or shows a notice in the keyboard.

Account and data

How do I delete my account?

Write to contact@smartype.in from your Google sign-in email address. Within 30 days we delete your SmarType account profile, AI usage count and subscription status from our servers.

How do I cancel Premium?

In Google Play: Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions → SmarType → Cancel.
