Questions

Is SmarType a Hindi keyboard?

Yes. Add Hindi in Languages and type in Hindi, with English kept beside it. The globe key switches between them, and Translate turns an English message into Hindi in one tap.

Can I type in Telugu, Tamil, Bengali or Urdu?

Yes. Add any of them in Languages. English stays in the list, so you switch with the globe key. Marathi, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi and Odia are available in Translate.

Does Translate work from English to Hindi and other Indian languages?

Yes. Translate writes in Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Urdu, English and 19 more languages. It detects the language you typed in.

Can I speak in Hindi and get text?

Yes. Tap the mic and speak in Hindi, English or a mix; SmarType writes it up with punctuation when you tap Check.

Is it free?

Typing in every language is free and needs no account. Sign in with Google for free AI tries for Translate, Rewrite and voice typing; Premium removes the limit.
