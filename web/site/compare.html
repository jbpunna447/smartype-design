Five differences that matter every day

Polite message rewrite in one tap

Pick Grammar fix, Polite, Professional, Casual, Simple or any of 22 built-in styles — or write your own custom style and pin up to 4 chips on the top bar.

Indian-language typing + 31-language AI translation

Switch between English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Bengali and 50+ keyboard layouts with the globe key. Tap Translate to send messages in 11 Indian languages, English and 19 more, inside WhatsApp or Gmail. Add the languages you use as one-tap chips.

Voice typing punctuation and cleanup

Speak for up to 60 seconds in memory and tap Check for punctuated text. Cancel deletes the audio immediately. Offline or out of tries, SmarType switches to Android system voice input.

Quick Notes in and over any app

Keep a scratchpad inside the keyboard or float a movable Quick Notes window above chats, browsers and forms, with one-tap AI polish and undo.

Strict password and everyday privacy

Everyday keystrokes stay on your phone without an account. Password and incognito fields shut off AI and voice dictation, and password fields also turn off autocorrect and the mic button. Read what stays on your phone.

Try SmarType free on Android 8.0+

Typing and suggestions need no account. Sign in with Google when you want free AI tries.
