Where SmarType is different

Fixing a message takes one tap

Most of us know what we want to say and just want it to sound right. Type it rough, tap the spark, pick Polite or Professional, and send. You stay in WhatsApp the whole time.

It speaks your languages

Gboard and SwiftKey are great at English. If you message in Hindi, Telugu or Tamil, or need to reply to someone in Spanish or Arabic, SmarType does it without leaving the chat.

Voice that waits while you think

Other voice typing stops when you pause. SmarType keeps listening for up to a minute and gives you back sentences, not a run-on line without full stops.

A notepad that floats

Copy an address, jot a list, keep a draft. The note window sits over whatever app you're in, and AI can tidy it if you ask.

It doesn't read over your shoulder

Your typing stays on your phone, and nothing is sent unless you tap an AI button. In password fields, all of it switches off. Here is exactly what stays on your phone.
