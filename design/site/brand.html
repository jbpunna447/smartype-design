Brand

One SmarType mark from the Play Store down to the status bar, one spark for AI, and one colour family from the app icon to every AI button.

The logo

A key: the S is its legend, the spark is its second function.

A white keycap on a vivid blue → cyan → mint gradient. The S has voice bars cut inside its tail, and the AI spark sits in the key's top-right corner, where real keys show what a long-press gives you. Typing, voice and AI in one mark.

On the brand gradient

On light and dark surfaces the icon stands out. On the brand gradient, or any bright colour, its own background blends in, so the inverse icon puts the same white key on a deep version of the same hues: #0B3A66 → #0A4B5A → #0C5A47, with a fainter light.

Normal: blends in
Inverse: stands out
Inverse, 56

Use the inverse icon (and lockup-on-gradient.svg) on the brand gradient and other bright colours: store art, the website hero, social images. Everywhere else, including the launcher, use the normal icon.

How it's built

PartSpec (units of the 108 canvas)
CanvasAndroid adaptive icon: 108 × 108; launchers show the middle 72 × 72 in their own shape
BackgroundLinear 135°: #1E9BD7 → #22C3CF (50%) → #4BDCA4, over the visible 72 × 72. Soft light: radial white from (34, 26), radius 46, 45% → 0%
KeyTop face x 31–77, y 29–75 (46 × 46), corner radius 11, white → #EDF3F5 top to bottom. Lip: the same shape 2.5 lower, #C3D5DA
SGoogle Sans Flex, weight 700, roundness 100, optical size 144, converted to a vector path (OFL). 29 tall, centred at (51, 54), Ink #08323B
Voice barsFour slots 1.1 wide with 1.1 bars between them, in the S's tail, starting 10% below its centre. A 0.9 rim is kept along the S's outline
SparkThe one spark (spark.svg) at 70%: its big star centred 13.5 right and 14 above the S's centre. Deep gradient #1257A8 → #0E8DA6 → #119A76 with a faint light
Themed iconThe key's silhouette with the S, voice bars and spark cut out; Android tints it
Status bar icon (24 dp)Same silhouette with a solid S: the bars would vanish at that size
Everything is vector. Key, lip, S, bars, spark and gradients are vector shapes. Only the 512 px Play Store icon and store art are exported as PNG, from the same vectors.

RuleSpec
ShapeAlways spark.svg: a big four-point star with curved sides and a small one at its top right. Never rotated, stretched or redrawn.
On the AI gradientInk #08323B. Used by the smartbar's AI buttons, the AI logo, selected style chips and the Premium badge.
Alone on light surfacesThe deep gradient #1257A8 → #0E8DA6 → #119A76: the logo's key, AI labels and headings, the website.
Alone on dark surfacesThe AI gradient #2AA8E0 → #4BDCA4.
SizeAt least 12 dp. In a round badge, the spark is 55% of the badge (20 dp in the 36 dp smartbar button).
SpaceKeep a quarter of the spark's width clear around it.
MeaningAI only. The AI gradient marks every AI action (Translate, Format and Mic keep their own icons on it); the spark marks AI writing and the brand. Clipboard, Notes and Emoji never use either.

Wordmark

Google Sans Flex at weight 600, the standard (not rounded) style, with the font's own S, outlined. It stays separate from the logo: the two only appear together in the lockup. The app still bundles DM Sans; moving the app to Google Sans Flex goes with this.
