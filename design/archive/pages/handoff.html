Rules that apply to every fix

  • Use the design-system tokens and components; no one-off colours or buttons.
  • One primary action per screen or panel; secondary actions are tonal or text. Task screens put it full width at the bottom.
  • Every panel and screen has the same way back: ‹ and system Back.
  • No internal words on screen (token, catalog, Firebase, Firestore, subtype, managed AI).
  • No log, analytics event or crash report carries typed text, candidates, clipboard, notes, audio or AI text.
  • Check each change at 360 dp and 412 dp, at large font, and in light, dark and AMOLED themes.
  • Lint adds no new finding; follow the test tiers and device test in AGENTS.md.
