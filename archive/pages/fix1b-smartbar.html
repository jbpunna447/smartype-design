Decisions for the owner

DecisionRecommendation
Where voice is transcribedStart with Android's on-device SpeechRecognizer: no server, no cost, no quota, audio stays on the phone. Cloud transcription (better mixed Hindi-English) goes through the Functions and the quota — a risky area that needs your plan review.
Does a voice message count as an AI use?No, while it's on-device. Revisit if it moves to the cloud.
Emoji on the smartbarOnly when there's room and the keyboard has no emoji key of its own (see Behaviour). Eight buttons don't fit 360 dp.
The smartbar is customisable todayUsers can reorder and pin quick actions now (QuickActionArrangement). Keep the main buttons fixed; move each user's other quick actions into ⋯ in their current order, once, on update.
Voice "Tidy up"Offer it as a setting, off by default, because it spends AI uses (the free allowance is a small one-time trial).
Suggestion row when emptyDecided (1 Nov): keep its height. Collapsing it would make the app above jump every time the field empties.
"Describe what to write" on a new noteYes, like Gmail's "Help me write". Uses the same AI quota as Rewrite. Can follow in a later release.

Acceptance criteria

  • The smartbar shows the same buttons in the same order in every field type; suggestions never replace it.
  • Rewrite, Translate, Format and Mic share one gradient style; Clipboard, Notes, Emoji and ⋯ are plain icons.
  • Tapping the Mic never opens another app or system window; recording happens in the smartbar.
  • Delete and Back during recording send nothing and insert nothing.
  • After ✓, the text is inserted at the cursor and can be undone for 6 s.
  • A recording with pauses of up to 10 s comes back as one text, in order.
  • Every panel header is ‹ and the panel name; ‹ and system Back return to the keys from each.
  • The smartbar stays visible while a panel is open, with the open panel's button highlighted; switching between Rewrite, Translate and Format is one tap and doesn't repeat a request for results already in memory.
  • Translate's language chips scroll inside the body and are never covered by the dock (B4).
  • Clipboard and Notes are separate buttons.
  • Every icon-only button (smartbar, panel headers, note editor) has a spoken label for TalkBack and shows its name on long-press (A8, K4).
  • The keyboard's height doesn't change between an empty and a non-empty field.
  • A note survives closing the keyboard, switching apps and restarting the phone.
  • While a note is open, typed keys go into the note, not the app behind it.
  • No log, analytics event or crash report carries audio, transcribed text, clipboard text or note text.
