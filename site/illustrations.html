The character

RuleSpec
CharacterThe brand key: white face, light grey lip. Two Ink eyes with a white catchlight, a smile, mint cheeks. No arms, no legs, no S
Where it comes fromThe app icon: the S lifts off the key and becomes the first letter typed, and the blank key wakes up. At the end the S flies home to the logo
ExpressionsWatching: eyes follow the action. Surprised: eyes wide, eyebrows up, a small round mouth. Curious: one eyebrow up. Focused: eyes squint, eyebrows down. Happy: ^ ^ eyes, big smile, a hop. Asleep: closed eyes
The sparkIts idea and its tool: it throws the spark to fix things. The deep spark gradient on light surfaces, the AI gradient on dark
Dark surfacesAnything drawn straight onto the background in Ink (voice bars, the magnifier) turns light; shapes on white cards and the key stay as they are
MotionSpringy: squash and stretch on hops, a blink every 4 s, loops of 3–9 s. Lottie in the app; when animations are off, the still frozen at the key moment
Words in scenesShort and translated with the app: one short message per bubble
Not illustratedAnything inside the keyboard, settings rows, dialogs and banners: they keep icons
