Brand review

One SmarType mark that works from the Play Store down to the status bar, one spark for AI, and colours that match what the app already uses.

What we have today

The problem. Three different marks and four different sparks. The icon a user taps is not the logo in the brand kit, and neither looks like the AI symbol inside the app. The earlier kit also proposed moving the app to blue, but the shipped icon, the AI gradient and the app's teal are already one cyan–teal family; the blue keycap is the odd one out.

Three directions

Same colours and the same S for all three, so the comparison is about the idea. Shown at real launcher sizes, as themed icons and in the status bar.

A · Keycap with the AI spark Recommended

A key: the S is its legend, the spark is its second function.

A white keycap with a thin light grey lip; the S (Google Sans Flex 700, round) has a tail cut into voice bars on a vivid blue → cyan → mint gradient with a soft light from the top left. On real keys the small symbol in the top corner is what a long-press gives you; here it's the AI spark, in a deeper version of the background's gradient, up in the key's top-right corner, so the icon says "keyboard with AI built in" in a keyboard's own language.

Works

  • Says "keyboard" at any size, and "AI" from 48 px up
  • The spark is part of the key, not a sticker: different from the floating sparkles other AI apps use
  • Written backwards, the S ends at its top tip and flows on into the spark: one movement, typing becoming AI
  • The S's tail turns into voice bars: typing, voice and AI in one mark
  • Simple vector; the themed icon is the key's silhouette with the S, its voice cuts and the spark cut out

Watch out

  • Below 32 px the spark becomes a dot and the voice cuts close up; the status-bar icon uses the solid S
  • Teal backgrounds are common for productivity apps

Chosen: the S's tail breaks into four even bars, like a voice level meter, so the mark says typing, voice and AI. The cuts read from 48 px up; the 24 dp status-bar icon uses the solid S, where the cuts would vanish. In motion, the bars can pulse in the splash and the voice bar.

The spark on the key

Spark in the gradient (chosen)
Spark in teal
Spark in ink
No spark

The spark uses a deeper version of the background's gradient (#1257A8 → #0E8DA6 → #119A76) with a faint light, so it stands out on the white key and still belongs with the glow around it. It sits in the key's top-right corner, just after the S's top tip. Teal and ink are calmer alternatives; without the spark the icon says nothing about AI.

The background gradient

Soft: today's Glacier → Seaglass
Vivid
Vivid with light (chosen)
Deep

Today's soft gradient reads as one flat colour. A wider shift, blue to cyan to mint, is how people recognise AI now, and it makes the white key stand out more, on light and dark home screens. The soft light from the top left gives the background depth, as if the key sits in a glow, while the key itself stays flat.

Everything is vector. The key, its lip, the S, the spark and the gradients are vector paths (SVG here; Android vector drawables in the app), so they stay sharp at every size. The S is the drawn monogram from today's icon (ic_smartype_foreground.xml), not a font. Only the 512 px Play Store icon and store art are exported as PNG, from these vectors. The 3D keycap from the earlier kit and the illustrations are the only raster art; the keycap is retired.

One spark shape everywhere (the path in spark.svg) and one blue → mint family. In the app, AI actions use ai-gradient-start #2AA8E0 → ai-gradient-end #4BDCA4 with Ink on top (5.1:1 and 7.9:1). On the white key, the spark uses a deeper version of the same colours so it stays visible. The three-star circle (ic_ai_logo) is retired.

Keep one spark: the two curved stars already on the smartbar's AI buttons. It appears in two places only: as the corner legend on the logo's key, and on AI actions in the app (Rewrite, Translate, Format, voice tidy-up, Premium). Retire the three-star circle (ic_ai_logo) and the straight star on today's icon, so there is one spark, and it always means AI.

Colour

Keep the shipped family. The icon, splash and store art use the vivid gradient with light above. Inside the app, AI actions move to the same blue → mint family (#2AA8E0 → #4BDCA4) with Ink icons and text; the teal tokens stay as they are.

After you choose

  • Final vector icon: adaptive foreground, background and monochrome layers, legacy icons, 512 px Play icon, debug and beta badges.
  • Notification icon, splash (with the key press or cursor animation), favicon and social image.
  • Wordmark and lockup files (SVG, PNG), clear space and minimum sizes.
  • A short brand page in the design system: logo, spark, colour, type, do and don't.
  • Then the illustrations, store screenshots, feature graphic and website use it.
