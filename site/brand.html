Type and icons

Google Sans Flex; one spark rule

Type, talk, rewrite.

Your keyboard, with AI that stays out of the way.

Body text at 15 sp. Hindi, English and numbers 0123456789 sit on the same line.

RoleSpec
TypefaceGoogle Sans Flex for the whole app, replacing DM Sans. Open Font License (OFL), so it can be bundled. It's a variable font: one file covers every weight. No second display face; hierarchy comes from size and weight.
How to bundleDownload from Google Fonts and add the variable TTF to res/font/ (for example google_sans_flex.ttf). Replace the eight dm_sans_*.ttf files and the FontFamily in app/apptheme/Type.kt. Bundle it rather than using downloadable fonts, so the app and keyboard look right offline and on first launch. Keep the OFL text in the open-source licences screen.
Weights400 Regular for body, 500 Medium for section titles and buttons, 700 Bold for screen titles. Avoid weights under 400 on small text.
ScriptsUse it for Latin text. For Devanagari and other scripts, Android falls back to the system font (Noto); check that line heights still match.
KeyboardKey labels follow the keyboard theme. Suggest Google Sans Flex for the built-in SmarType themes too, so the app and keyboard match.
ScaleTitle 22/28 bold · Section 17/24 medium · Body 15/22 regular · Caption 13/18 regular · Key labels follow the keyboard theme
IconsMaterial Symbols Rounded, outline, 24 dp, weight 400. Filled only for the selected state.
Spark ✦Only on AI actions and AI entry points (toolbar AI key, Rewrite, voice clean-up, Premium). Never as decoration on non-AI screens.
Waveform barsThe S's bars become the Voice mode level meter, so dictation feels native to the brand.
Usage

Do and don't

Do

  • Show the keycap whole, with its 3D edge and shadow, on white or very light surfaces.
  • On dark surfaces (splash in dark mode), use the keycap as-is; its rim separates it from the background.
  • Keep clear space around it of at least 25% of its width.
  • Use the monochrome silhouette wherever only one colour is possible (notifications, themed icons).

Don't

  • Go above 50% in the adaptive icon: on circle launchers the keycap corners get cut.
  • Put the keycap on a coloured or busy background inside the icon.
  • Recolour the S or the spark, or add more sparks.
  • Use the spark gradient for normal buttons.

Notification small icon: Android needs a flat white silhouette at 24 dp. Use the monochrome S + spark, simplified (drop the thinnest bars below 2 dp). A vector version of the S and spark should be traced from the master artwork for crisp results; the PNGs here are good enough for review and the Play listing.
