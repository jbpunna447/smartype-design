Setup: a working keyboard first, sign-in when it's worth it

Today a new user reads four generic pages, a legal paragraph, then has to decide on sign-in before SmarType even types. The new order gets the keyboard working in under a minute, lets people try it, and asks for sign-in when they reach for AI.

Acceptance criteria

  • A new user can enable and choose SmarType without seeing a sign-in screen.
  • Onboarding has at most two intro pages and no "Page X of Y" text.
  • No onboarding screen mentions Firebase, Firestore or "account-backed features".
  • Each screen has one full-width primary button; secondary actions are text links.
  • Setup cards show status chips, not switches; the action button is inside the active card.
  • Returning from Android settings advances setup without a tap.
  • Skipping sign-in leaves a working keyboard; signing in later from ✦ works the same way.
  • After signing in, the user sees "Signed in as …" and never a "Sign in" message.
