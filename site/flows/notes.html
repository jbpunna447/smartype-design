Notes: write with AI

A private place to draft over the app: write yourself, or tell AI what you need and keep refining it in conversation, then insert.

New screens are drawn at real size: 1 px = 1 dp on a 360 × 800 dp phone. Open "Spec" under any screen for sizes, tokens and icons.

The problem

Quick Notes has five names for one feature (N1), says "Saved" while you're still typing and empties the editor when you tap Save (N2), and notes can't be copied, shared or improved with AI (N3).

More importantly, it doesn't help with the job people open it for: getting a message right before sending it.

What people need

NeedWhy
Draft in private, away from the chatPeople draft in a notes app for privacy and distance: no "typing…" indicator, and time to think before sending.
Start from an intention, not a blank page"Reply to Ravi, thank him, ask for the receipt" is easier than writing it.
Refine by saying what to change"Add that I'll pay on Friday", "warmer", "shorter": a few rounds, like a conversation.
Change the whole thing at onceMake all of it more polite, shorter, or turn it into a list, without retyping.
Give AI the message they're replying toThe keyboard can't read the other app's screen; copying the message is the natural way to hand it over.
Stay in controlNothing replaces their words unless they choose; they can compare versions and undo.
Come back later and insertKeep a draft over days, then put it into the app in one tap.

What others do

ProductHow it worksWhat we take

Edge cases

CaseWhat happens
No notes yet"Your notes show up here" with New note.
The "display over other apps" permission isn't grantedFirst time: a short explanation and Allow. If refused, the note opens in the SmarType app instead.
Send with an empty Ask AI boxSend is disabled.
The user edits the note while a draft is comingThe draft still arrives; Replace note asks "Replace your latest text?"
AI can't help with the requestThe card says "I can't help with that one. Try asking differently." No use is counted.
Offline, out of tries or signed outSame banners as AI rewrite, inside the overlay; writing by hand still works.
A very long note or copied messageOver the server limit: "This is too long for AI. Select part of it."
Close the overlay with unused draftsThe note is saved; the drafts are discarded (they were never part of the note).
Copied text is a password or other sensitive clip"Use copied text" isn't offered.
Insert into a password fieldInsert is disabled with "Can't insert into a password field".
Delete a noteSnackbar "Note deleted · Undo" for 5 s.
The process is killed while typingAt most the last 800 ms of typing is lost.
Rotate the phoneThe overlay keeps the text, the drafts and the cursor.

Done when

  • "Quick Note", "scratchpad", "Scratch draft" and "Saved note" no longer appear anywhere.
  • AI never changes the note until the user taps Use this, Replace note or Add below; Undo restores the previous text.
  • Every Ask AI send and every quick chip is one AI use; switching versions costs nothing.
  • Copied text is only sent when the user taps "Use copied text"; sensitive clips are never offered.
  • With text selected, Ask AI and the smartbar's tools act on the selection only.
  • After an update, every existing note appears with its text unchanged.
  • A note survives closing the keyboard, switching apps and restarting the phone.
  • Insert adds text only at the cursor and changes nothing else in the field.
  • Notes stay on the phone (Room database); no note text, instruction or draft in logs, analytics or crash reports.
