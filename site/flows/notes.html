Notes

A quick place for anything: a list, something to keep, a message to get right. AI helps when you ask, and the note is always yours.

New screens are drawn at real size: 1 px = 1 dp on a 360 × 800 dp phone. Open "Spec" under any screen for sizes, tokens and icons.

The problem

Quick Notes has five names for one feature (N1), says "Saved" while you're still typing and empties the editor when you tap Save (N2), and notes can't be copied, shared or improved with AI (N3).

People open a notes space for many small jobs: a grocery list, an address or order number they copied, a message they want to get right before sending. It has to be instant for all of them, and helpful with AI without getting in the way.

What people need

NeedWhy
Jot something down instantlyLists, numbers, ideas: open, type or speak, done. No title, no Save button.
Keep something they copiedAn order number, an address, a message: paste and it's kept.
Draft in private before sendingPeople draft away from the chat for privacy and distance: no "typing…" indicator, and time to think.
Ask AI in their own words"Add that I'll pay on Friday", "make it casual", "group by aisle": one sentence, the note updates.
One-tap help that fits the noteA list wants "group by aisle"; a message wants "shorter" or "fix grammar".
Stay in controlEvery AI change can be undone in one tap.
Use it anywhereInsert the note into the app they're in, or copy and share it.

Done when

  • Opening a new note puts the cursor in it with the keyboard up: zero taps before typing.
  • "Quick Note", "scratchpad", "Scratch draft" and "Saved note" no longer appear anywhere.
  • Every AI change can be undone in one tap, and Undo restores the exact previous text.
  • Each Ask AI send or suggestion chip is one AI use; showing suggestions costs nothing.
  • With text selected, Ask AI and the smartbar's tools act on the selection only.
  • After an update, every existing note appears with its text unchanged.
  • A note survives closing the keyboard, switching apps and restarting the phone.
  • Insert adds text only at the cursor and changes nothing else in the field.
  • Notes stay on the phone (Room database); no note text or instruction in logs, analytics or crash reports.
