Design system › Tokens

Tokens

Values are dp in Android, shown as px. Switch the theme at the top right to see light and dark.

Colour

primary
Main actions, selected state, links, focus ring, the active step. Reads 10.5:1 on background in dark, 5.4:1 in light.

on-primary
Text and icons on a primary fill (filled buttons, selected chips). 7.5:1 in dark, 5.7:1 in light.

primary-container
Soft emphasis: tonal buttons, selected list rows, info banners, the current setup step.

on-primary-container
Text and icons on primary-container. 7.6:1 in dark, 10.1:1 in light.

inverse-primary
Action text inside a snackbar on inverse-surface.

secondary
Positive confirmation: 'Keyboard ready', 'Saved', success ticks. Never the main action colour.

on-secondary
Text on a secondary fill.

secondary-container
Success chips and banners ('✓ SmarType is on').

on-secondary-container
Text on secondary-container.

tertiary
Neutral-cool accent for secondary information: plan badges, metadata icons.

on-tertiary
Text on a tertiary fill.

tertiary-container
Neutral badges such as 'Free plan', 'Coming soon'.

on-tertiary-container
Text on tertiary-container.

background
The page behind everything. Graphite-teal in dark, porcelain in light, true black on AMOLED.

on-background
Primary text on background. 15.3:1 in dark, 14.6:1 in light.

surface
Same as background; the base the surface-container steps sit on.

on-surface
Titles and body text on any surface or surface-container.

surface-variant
Track of switches and sliders, unselected chip fill.

on-surface-variant
Secondary text: row summaries, captions, helper text. 7.0:1 on surface-container-highest in dark, 5.4:1 in light.

surface-container-lowest
Text fields and inputs that sit inside a card.

surface-container-low
Grouped settings cards, list groups.

surface-container
Default card, top app bar when scrolled, bottom sheets.

surface-container-high
Dialogs, menus, the raised card in a group.

surface-container-highest
Search fields, segmented control track, the highest tonal step.

surface-bright
Pressed and hovered surfaces in dark mode.

surface-dim
Behind a modal sheet in light mode.

outline
Borders that carry meaning: outlined buttons, text field borders, focus-less inputs. 6.3:1 in dark, 4.2:1 in light.

outline-variant
Hairline dividers between rows. Decorative only, never the only sign of a control.

inverse-surface
Snackbars and toasts.

inverse-on-surface
Text on inverse-surface.

error
Error text and icons, destructive actions (Sign out, Delete). Always paired with words.

on-error
Text on an error fill.

error-container
Error banners.

on-error-container
Text on error-container.

warning-container
Warning banner: something is limited, but nothing failed.

on-warning-container
Text and icons on warning-container.

scrim
Behind dialogs and sheets, at 32% opacity.

brand-cyan
Keyboard AI gradient start (SmarTypeCyan). Keyboard and brand art only.

brand-teal
Keyboard AI gradient end (SmarTypeTeal). Keyboard and brand art only.

ai-gradient-start
App AI gradient start (SmarTypeAppGradient). The brand's blue → mint family, matching the app icon. Only on AI actions: Rewrite, Translate, voice Done, Premium.

ai-gradient-end
App AI gradient end.

on-ai-gradient
Text and icons on either AI gradient, in every theme. 5.1:1 at the app gradient's start, 7.9:1 at its end, 7.5:1 on the keyboard gradient.
